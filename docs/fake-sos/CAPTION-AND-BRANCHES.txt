TWO POSSIBLE FUTURE BRANCHES — narrative notes only, no Episode2 produced
A—BOOST: The astronaut chooses a risky pursuit. A single burst may reach the ship's docking handle, but missing leaves no power. Wangdoo provides the opportunity; the astronaut chooses the route and acts.
B—SHIELD: The astronaut protects both companions as the barrier fails. The ship escapes. Stranded, they must use the station's real tools and learn how to make a safe communications repair after the brief shield expires.
No branch implies an automatic rescue, limitless energy or a power that chooses for the explorer.

# Episode 1 production gates

The four actual character renders are preserved in the delivery. Wangdoo uses
sculpted, articulated cloth surfaces, a baked woven normal texture, a distinct
gray head and white pear-shaped torso. His height is 0.763 m beside a 1.953 m
astronaut. The suit is matte black with an opaque visor.

The definitive plush photograph was not attached to the current conversation.
The model is provisional from the detailed written description and the earlier
robot-penguin image. An exact photographic likeness has **not** been verified.

A real 450-frame opening MP4 was rendered and its motion frames inspected.
Focused corrections widened the docking view, centered the penguin, removed
blocked cameras and corrected the door vibration's non-finite calculation.
The backpack-check approach was changed to reach a real pack loop from behind
the plush rather than crossing its head. The corrected 450-frame geometry check
reports zero intersections, less than 0.3 mm planted-foot drift and numerical
contact error below 0.001 mm. This is a stylized small-team 3D production, not a
claim of feature-film animation quality.

The narrator is the stock American male Kokoro am_michael voice, synthesized
locally without paid services. Timing and audio signal are inspectable; this
environment cannot independently listen to the reference videos or evaluate
the final voice by ear. Reference access limitations must remain in final QC.

# DON'T OPEN THE DOOR — Episode 1 reset

One explorer, one familiar guide, one call for help. The station looks recently
occupied. A locked room knocks back, but Wangdoo's scanner finds nobody. Then
the scanner fails. A live view of the explorer's own ship reveals a stranger
starting its departure. Opening the door takes thirty seconds. The ship leaves
in thirty seconds. Save a possible person, or protect the only way home?

The film ends before either choice is taken. There is no time loop, duplicate
ship, alternate astronaut, unexplained project or unrelated monster. The same
three knocks connect the opening, the scan and the final choice. The ship is
recognizable by its window, seat and personal red mug. The explorer and Wangdoo
remain visibly in the station while the clearly labelled LIVE ship feed plays.

Wangdoo offers a map and an observation. His tool can fail. His small worried
reaction makes him a vulnerable companion rather than an answer machine. The
astronaut checks the pack, takes the risk seriously and must decide. An authentic
Lost Sensor activity turns that same failed tool into a reason to explore AltWing.

Only Episode 1 is produced. The longer emotional direction is preserved:
Wangdoo initially helps the explorer find possible paths. Experience teaches the
explorer to observe, try and choose independently. Much later, the explorer uses
those learned skills to protect and repair Wangdoo. Both survive. The earned
final message may be: “He never gave you wings. He helped you find your own.”
That payoff is not explained or shown in this episode.

A means trying the manual release and risking a stranded rescue; B means
returning now to stop the departure and leaving the room's occupant unknown.
Neither has been chosen by the audience. Subsequent writing must preserve the
cost of the actual winning choice. No additional episode is rendered now.

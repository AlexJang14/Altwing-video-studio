# Wangdoo — modular Wing character bible

Wangdoo is a warm, plush-like AI exploration companion: gray round head and
hanging flippers, white face and belly, curved eyes, yellow beak and short feet.
The plush body, tiny backpack and recognizable silhouette stay consistent.
Wings unfold from the pack; they never replace his cloth body or flippers.
The original plush photograph has not reached this workspace. Exact photographic
likeness remains UNVERIFIED; the supplied description and preserved model inform
this revision. Do not invent a photographic comparison.

The astronaut is a young explorer discovering his own path. Wangdoo offers
information and possible paths. The astronaut chooses, acts, learns and grows.

## Reusable rules

`src/characters/wings/system.ts` is the canonical catalog and deterministic event
replay system. Energy, active identity, activation cost, duration, cooldown,
interference and rejected activations are explicit. One Wing at a time; no free
power swaps. Model sockets are driven by state, not independent visual toggles.
`socket.ts` renders the compact attachment through the pack socket. Only Scout's
appearance is authored/revealed on screen in this episode; catalog descriptions
of future forms are production constraints, not completed future-episode assets.
All values are fictional story units, not engineering performance claims.
Carry remaining energy and cooldown history across continuous story time. Show a
charging opportunity before replenishing energy; do not silently reset it for a
convenient new Wing reveal. The canonical replay accepts an initial energy budget.

## Future story discipline

Introduce a new Wing only when a specific obstacle makes its capability useful.
Show its recognizable form, what activation costs, what it cannot do, and the
astronaut's consequential decision. Existing knowledge and materials still
matter. Never equip a new Wing simply to erase danger. Each reveal is a special
event, not a checklist or arbitrary power-up. Future episodes can contrast
curiosity, care, courage, insight, understanding and creativity while the
astronaut gradually discovers his own wings. No later episode is produced here.

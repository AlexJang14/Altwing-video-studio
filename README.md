# The Last Wing — rebuilt episodes

Both movies use a softer, rounded Wangdoo with expressive eyes, tiny yellow
feet, gold-edged navy flippers and cyan circuitry. The astronaut keeps his
matte-black suit and sealed visor. Movement, teamwork, physical comedy,
survival stakes and the AltWing endings have been expanded.

- [Episode 1 — The Last Signal · 58 seconds](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AlexJang14/Altwing-video-studio/cinematic-rebuild-delivery/the-last-wing-episode-01-final.mp4)
- [Episode 2 — We Made a Mistake · Planet Route B · 59 seconds](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AlexJang14/Altwing-video-studio/cinematic-rebuild-delivery/the-last-wing-episode-02-planet-route-final.mp4)

Download an MP4 and open it in your phone's or computer's video player if the
browser does not play it inline. Both final movies are native 1080 × 1920,
30 fps, H.264/Rec.709 with stereo AAC, English voices and burned-in captions.

Episode 2 is explicitly conditional: this is what happens **if** the planet
route is chosen. It does not claim an audience vote has already occurred.

The endings show authentic captures from the official [AltWing
website](https://junjang06.github.io/altwing/#/). They promote real engineering
exploration and browser projects; the alien survival missions are fictional.
LINK IN BIO assumes the publisher puts the official URL in their social bio.

Voices are generated locally with a public-domain LJ Speech model, using
consistent narrator and Wangdoo treatments. They are synthetic voices, not
professional human performances. Music and effects are original synthesis.
No paid voice or rendering API was used. The project retains the prior working
episodes and their reusable GLB assets.

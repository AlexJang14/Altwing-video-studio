The native 1080×1920 export is rendering. It will be added here after its final
validation.

Wangdoo keeps the original reference's happy curved eyes, yellow beak and feet,
blush, navy robot flippers, gold trim, cyan details, and back thruster. His new
model has a rounder plush body, a connected ivory face and belly, fine fabric
texture, cleaner armor, and closer, brighter shots.

This episode is caption-led, with original music, mechanical chirps, footsteps,
wind, landing effects, and the flower and gateway's reply. Spoken voices are
not included. Both episodes and their render commands are included in the
source project. In Remotion Studio, choose `TheLastWing-Episode2`.

The project ZIP includes the generated audio for both episodes. The final
choice asks whether to enter the gate or return to the ship.
